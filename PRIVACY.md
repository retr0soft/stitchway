# Stitchway privacy policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Stitchway is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device
The app stores the following locally, and only on your device:
- your settings (theme, sound, haptics, reduce motion, colour-blind palette),
- your solved puzzles, times and daily streak,
- a puzzle you left half-finished, so you can continue it.

This data is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Android's own backup may copy app data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on; that is controlled by your device settings, not by Stitchway.

## What the app does not do
- It has no account system and asks for no personal details.
- It asks for no permissions and has no network access.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

## The share button
"Share result" opens Android's share sheet with a short text that you can send through an app of your choice. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send it, and what happens next is governed by the app you pick.

## Future versions
If a later version adds ads or a purchase to remove them, this policy will be updated before release to describe exactly what those services collect.

## Children
Stitchway is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
